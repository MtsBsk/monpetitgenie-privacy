Privacy Policy

Mon Petit Génie — last updated: 09/08/2026

Data controller

You, as the parent or legal guardian of the tracked child, are responsible for the data entered into the app for your personal tracking use. Mon Petit Génie provides the technical tool and does not use this data for any other purpose.

Data collected

The child's first name and age, daily journal entries (mood, behavior, concentration, energy, free notes), tracked medications and dosing history, history of treatment additions/discontinuations. No data is collected about parents beyond an optional email used only to recover the account.

Purpose and legal basis

This data includes health data of a minor (a special category under Article 9 of the GDPR). It is processed solely to help you track your child's daily life and generate a report for healthcare professionals, based on your explicit consent as holder of parental authority (Article 9.2.a GDPR), collected when the profile is created.

Hosting and security

Data is stored locally on your device, and, if you secure your account with an email, synchronized to Supabase servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt region, eu-west-1). Each account only has access to its own data (technical isolation at the database level).

Retention period

Data is kept as long as the profile exists in the app. You can delete it entirely at any time from Settings → Danger zone.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port this data. Export (JSON format) and complete deletion are available directly in Settings, without needing to request it elsewhere.

Sharing with third parties

Data is never sold or used for advertising purposes. The PDF report generated from the Report tab is only shared with recipients you choose yourself (e.g. the child's doctor) via your phone's native sharing.

Contact

For any question about your data or this policy, contact us at the address listed on the app's App Store listing.
